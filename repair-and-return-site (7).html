Company

About Repair & Return

A true end‑to‑end return management solutions company.

Repair & Return serves international and domestic manufacturers with reverse logistics, RMA and technical services, including repair, for a whole range of industry segments, with customised solutions that lower RMA costs.

We are transparent by design. We share knowledge and walk customers through the process, approach and methods used at every stage of the engagement.

Vision

Make returns cheaper, faster and fully transparent for the manufacturers who trust us.
